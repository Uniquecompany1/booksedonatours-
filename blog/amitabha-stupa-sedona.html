Amitabha Stupa & Peace Park: Sedona's free hidden sanctuary

Tucked into a piñon-juniper hillside in West Sedona, at the base of Thunder Mountain, sits a 36-foot Buddhist stupa that most Sedona visitors drive right past — and it's one of the best free things in town.

What you're looking at

A stupa is a sacred structure in the Tibetan Buddhist tradition — a place for meditation, prayer and circumambulation (walking around it clockwise, which is believed to generate merit and peace). The Amitabha Stupa was built under the guidance of Tibetan masters and filled with sacred relics and offerings. Prayer flags ring the site, and a set of prayer wheels stands nearby — spin them clockwise as you pass, as visitors have done in the Himalayas for centuries.

How to visit

  • Cost: free. Donations are accepted and help maintain the park.
  • Hours: open daily, dawn to dusk. Go early morning for the quietest visit and the best light on the red rocks behind it.
  • Getting there: it's in West Sedona, a short drive from Highway 89A, with a small parking area and a short uphill walk on a dirt path. Wear real shoes.
  • Etiquette: this is an active sacred site, not a photo backdrop. Walk clockwise around the stupa, keep voices low, and be respectful of people meditating. Photos are fine; climbing on the stupa is not.
  • Time needed: 30–60 minutes is right. Sit on one of the benches, spin the wheels, walk the loop three times as tradition suggests, and just be quiet for a bit.

Why we send people here

Sedona's vortex sites can come with a lot of commercial noise. The stupa is the opposite: no ticket, no pitch, no schedule — just a genuinely peaceful place that locals protect fiercely. If you're deciding whether Sedona's spiritual side is for you, spend an hour here before you book anything. It's the honest test.

And if it resonates, several of the vortex tours we book include the stupa with a guide who can go deeper into its history and practice — the Vortex & Chakra Tour among them. Or make it the quiet bookend to a louder day: jeep tour in the morning, stupa at golden hour.
