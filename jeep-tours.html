How to choose

  • Want the famous one? Broken Arrow. It's exclusive to Pink Jeep, it sells out first, and it's the ride people describe at dinner for a year. Book it 2–3 days ahead in spring and fall — longer on peak weekends.
  • Traveling with kids or grandparents? Red Rock Range (mild) or the private Lil' Rattler, where the guide sets the pace for your group only.
  • Want maximum thrill? Diamondback Gulch or the Broken Arrow / Scenic Rim combo. Skip these with back, neck or pregnancy concerns — the roughness ratings are honest.
  • Care about photos more than adrenaline? Scenic Rim climbs to 2,000-foot overlooks; Red Rock Range at sunset is the photographer's pick.
