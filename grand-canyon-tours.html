Grand Canyon or Antelope Canyon — which day trip?

  • First time in Arizona, one day only: Grand Canyon. It's the icon for a reason.
  • Photographers: Antelope Canyon's light beams are the shot — but it books out further ahead.
  • Two free days: Do both. They're in opposite directions and completely different experiences.
  • Mobility concerns: Grand Canyon tours are mostly viewpoint-based; Antelope Canyon involves ladders and uneven walking.
