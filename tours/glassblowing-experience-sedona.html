Glassblowing Experience in Sedona

★★★★★ 5.0 from 523 reviews · Sedona, Arizona

About this tour

The most hands-on thing you can book in Sedona: two hours in a working glass studio, where an instructor walks you through gathering molten glass from the furnace, shaping it, and adding color — and you leave with a piece you actually made. No experience needed; the pros handle the dangerous parts and coach the rest.

It holds a perfect 5.0 rating from more than 500 guests, and it's the rare Sedona activity that works on a too-hot afternoon, a rainy day, or for the traveler who has already done the jeep thing.

What's included

  • 2-hour hands-on glassblowing session
  • One-on-one instruction from a glass artist
  • Your own finished glass piece
  • All materials and safety equipment

Good to know

  • Finished glass has to cool slowly in the annealer — plan on next-day pickup or ask the studio about shipping your piece home.
  • Wear natural fibers and closed-toe shoes; tie back long hair.
