Scenic Rim Jeep Tour

Operated by Pink Jeep Tours · The big-views tour

About this tour

Most Sedona jeep tours stay in the valley. The Scenic Rim climbs out of it — up the old wagon road toward the Mogollon Rim, roughly 2,000 feet above the valley floor, to overlooks where Sedona spreads out below you and the San Francisco Peaks fill the northern horizon.

The route passes the formations locals name like old friends — Snoopy Rock, Cow Pies, the Teacup — before the climb. It's a moderate ride: enough dirt-road character to feel like an adventure, none of the rock-crawling drama of Broken Arrow. Photographers and view-hunters, this is yours.

What's included

  • Guided jeep tour climbing to Mogollon Rim overlooks
  • Stops for panoramic photos above the Sedona valley
  • Professional local guide

Good to know

  • Ride intensity: moderate — bumpy dirt roads, no extreme rock sections.
  • Can't choose between views and rock-crawling? The Broken Arrow / Scenic Rim Combo does both in 3 hours.
