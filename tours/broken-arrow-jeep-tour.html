Broken Arrow Jeep Tour

Operated by Pink Jeep Tours · Sedona, Arizona

About this tour

Broken Arrow is the most famous jeep trail in Sedona — a roughly 4-mile slickrock route that climbs over smooth red-rock fins to Chicken Point and Submarine Rock, then descends the "Road of No Return," a natural stone staircase that has to be seen to be believed. Pink Jeep Tours is the only commercial operator the Forest Service permits on this trail, and they've been running it since 1960.

Expect real off-roading: steep slickrock climbs and descents in a custom open-air Jeep Wrangler holding six to nine passengers, with two stops to get out, walk the rock and take photos. Your guide narrates the geology, the Western film history and the plant life along the way.

What's included

  • 2-hour guided off-road jeep tour of the Broken Arrow Trail
  • Professional local guide
  • Stops at Chicken Point and Submarine Rock
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour

Good to know

  • Ride intensity: bumpy (3 of 5). Not recommended for travelers with back or neck problems or during pregnancy.
  • This is Sedona's most popular tour and it sells out — book 2–3 days ahead in spring and fall, a week ahead for peak weekends.
  • Confirmation is received at booking; bring your confirmation on your phone.
