Private Antelope Canyon & Horseshoe Bend Experience

Full day · Private — your group only

About this tour

The private version of the Sedona-to-Page run: your own vehicle and guide for the day, photo stops when you want them, and a schedule that flexes around your group instead of a bus manifest. You'll still enter Antelope Canyon with a Navajo guide — that's required for everyone, and it's part of what makes the visit good — but everything around the canyon is yours to shape.

For photographers chasing specific light, families with their own rhythm, and groups of five or more doing the per-person math, private is usually the right call.

What's included

  • Private vehicle and guide for the full day
  • Antelope Canyon visit with a Navajo guide
  • Horseshoe Bend overlook, photo stops on request

Good to know

  • Priced per group — total confirmed at booking based on group size and canyon section.
  • Walking of about 1.5 miles on uneven ground still applies; private doesn't change the canyon.
