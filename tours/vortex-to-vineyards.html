Vortex to Vineyards — with Rachel Reenstra

About this tour

Every other tour on this site is a format. This one is a person. Rachel Reenstra — Emmy-nominated host of ABC's Wildlife Docs and a Sedona local — runs a tour that starts at a vortex site and ends at the vineyards, and somewhere in between becomes the thing reviewers describe with words like "life-changing" and "I laughed until I cried."

There's music. There's a singing dog, Willie, who features in a suspicious number of five-star reviews. There's real insight about the land and the wine. It's the least generic experience we sell, and the one people book a second time with friends in tow.

What's included

  • 3–4 hour hosted experience: vortex site + Verde Valley wineries
  • Hosted personally by Rachel Reenstra
  • Small, personal group format

Good to know

  • Availability is limited by Rachel's schedule — this one genuinely sells out. Book ahead.
  • Guests must be 21+ for wine tasting.
