Sedona Ebike Tour — Self-Guided

★★★★★ 4.8 from 685 reviews · Your pace, no guide schedule

About this tour

Some travelers don't want a guide, a group or a timetable — they want a bike and a map. This self-guided ebike tour hands you a quality electric bike and a proven route through Sedona's scenery, and the motor flattens the hills that make Sedona cycling hard work. Two hours and fifteen minutes of riding exactly as fast — or as slowly — as you like.

At 4.8 stars from nearly 700 reviews, the equipment and the route both get consistent praise. It's the most independent way we sell to see Sedona.

What's included

  • Electric bike rental with self-guided Sedona route
  • About 2 hours 15 minutes of ride time
  • Free cancellation

Good to know

  • You should be comfortable riding a bike in traffic-adjacent areas; the ebike does the climbing, not the steering.
  • Start early in summer — Sedona afternoons are hot, and morning light on the rocks is better anyway.
