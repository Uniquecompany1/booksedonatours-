Private Wine Tour from Sedona

Private — your group only · Customizable itinerary

About this tour

Your wineries, your order, your pace. A private wine tour builds the Verde Valley day around your group's taste — the big reds at one estate, the creekside patio at another, a stop in Jerome if that's the mood — with a private driver-guide who knows which tasting rooms pour generously and which patios catch the sunset.

It's the standard choice for bachelorettes, couples traveling together and corporate groups, because the day is an event you design rather than a schedule you join.

What's included

  • Private vehicle and driver-guide
  • Customizable winery itinerary
  • Door-to-door service from Sedona

Good to know

  • Guests must be 21+ to taste; tasting fees vary by winery unless your booking states otherwise.
  • Priced per person with group-size variation — total confirmed at booking.
