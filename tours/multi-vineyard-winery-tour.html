Verde Valley Wine Tour — 4 Vineyards

★★★★★ 4.6 from 822 reviews · Limo-style bus · Verde Valley

About this tour

The Verde Valley's greatest-hits wine day: four vineyards in a limo-style bus, with a charcuterie board along the way and a host who handles the driving, the route and the reservations. Six to seven hours, Sedona pickup, and the best price per winery of any wine tour we list.

The Verde Valley's scene is compact enough to cover properly in a day: estate vineyards along Oak Creek, tasting rooms in Cottonwood's old town, and pours that routinely surprise people who didn't know Arizona made wine at all.
