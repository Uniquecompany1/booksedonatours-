Stargazing with Telescopes & Video Astronomy

Deep-sky viewing · Video astronomy setup

About this tour

Classic stargazing has a bottleneck: one eyepiece, one person at a time, and deep-sky objects that look like faint smudges. Video astronomy removes it. A camera on the telescope stacks light in real time and shows the whole group galaxies, nebulae and star clusters on screen — in color, in detail, together.

Under Sedona's Dark Sky skies, it's the deepest look into the universe any tour here offers. Astronomy people love it; beginners love it more, because for once they can actually see what everyone's pointing at.

What's included

  • Telescope observing session at a dark site
  • Video astronomy — deep-sky objects on screen for the whole group
  • Knowledgeable astronomy guides

Good to know

  • Best around the new moon; book your first clear night as a weather buffer.
  • Bring warm layers — you'll be outside and mostly still.
