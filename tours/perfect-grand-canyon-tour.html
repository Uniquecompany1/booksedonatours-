About this tour

Naming your tour "perfect" is a bold move. Four hundred-plus reviewers at 4.8 stars suggest this one earns it. The formula is simple: a full day at the South Rim with guides who are genuine canyon experts — the kind who know which viewpoint is empty at 11am, where the light hits at 3pm, and the stories that make a big hole in the ground feel personal.

From Sedona, the day runs through Oak Creek Canyon and the high desert to the rim, with multiple viewpoints and time in Grand Canyon Village. If you have exactly one day for the canyon, this is the tour we point people to first.

What's included

  • Full-day Grand Canyon South Rim tour from Sedona
  • Local expert guide throughout
  • Multiple rim viewpoints and Grand Canyon Village time
  • Free cancellation

Good to know

  • It's a full day — dress in layers; the rim sits near 7,000 feet and runs much cooler than Sedona.
  • Mostly viewpoint-based walking, suitable for most fitness levels.
