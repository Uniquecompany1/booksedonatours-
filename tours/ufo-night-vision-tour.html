About this tour

Here's the thing nobody tells you about the night sky: most of what's moving up there is invisible to the naked eye. Put on a pair of night vision goggles in a Dark Sky community and the sky turns busy — satellites crossing in every direction, shooting stars you'd never have caught, and the occasional light that doesn't behave like either.

This tour equips every guest with night vision gear and a guide who knows the regular traffic from the strange. It's the most "whoa" per minute of any night tour we sell.

What's included

  • Night vision goggles for every guest
  • Guided sky-watching with UFO and satellite identification

Good to know

  • Pairs with the Original UFO Tour for the full Sedona-weird experience — but one night tour per night is plenty.
