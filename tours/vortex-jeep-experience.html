About this tour

Can't decide between a jeep tour and a vortex tour? This is the hybrid: Sedona's four famous energy sites — Airport Mesa, Bell Rock, Cathedral Rock and Boynton Canyon — reached by jeep over gentle back roads, with a guide who covers both the scenery and the spiritual side of each stop.

The jeep format solves the practical problem with vortex-hopping on your own: trailhead parking at the famous sites fills by mid-morning. On this tour, that's the guide's problem, not yours.

What's included

  • Jeep transport to four major vortex sites
  • Guide covering geology, history and the energy-site tradition
  • Gentle roads — this is the mild version of a jeep tour

Good to know
