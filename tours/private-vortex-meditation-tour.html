Private Vortex Tour with Guided Meditation

About this tour

Most vortex tours are heavy on information. This private version is heavy on silence — in the good way. Your guide takes you to Sedona's energy sites and leads a guided meditation at each one, with the talking kept to what serves the sitting.

It's the choice for experienced meditators who want the sites without the tour-bus energy, and for anyone whose ideal Sedona morning is quiet, early light and red rock. Private format means the meditation length is set by your practice, not a schedule.

What's included

  • Private vortex site visits — your group only
  • Guided meditation at the sites
  • Unhurried, quiet pacing

Good to know

  • Early departures are the move — the sites are emptiest and the light is best.
  • New to meditation? The guide leads everything; no experience assumed.
