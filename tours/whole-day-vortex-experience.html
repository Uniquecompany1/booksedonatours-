Whole-Day Sedona Vortex Experience

About this tour

Sedona's four famous vortex sites each have a different character — Airport Mesa's panoramic lift, Bell Rock's steady strength, Cathedral Rock's feminine calm, Boynton Canyon's balance of both. Most tours make you pick one or two. This full-day experience does the complete circuit, with real time at each site instead of a photo stop.

If the vortexes are the reason you came to Sedona — not a side trip from the reason — this is your tour. You'll finish the day knowing which site is yours, which is knowledge most visitors never get.

What's included

  • Full-day guided circuit of all four major vortex sites
  • Time for meditation and exploration at each stop
  • Local spiritual guide

Good to know

  • Short hikes at several sites — wear real shoes and bring water and sun protection.
  • Short on time? The Vortex Odyssey is the top-rated shorter version.
