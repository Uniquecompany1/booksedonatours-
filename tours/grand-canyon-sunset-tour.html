Grand Canyon Sunset Tour from Sedona

Full day · Timed for golden hour

About this tour

Ask any canyon photographer when the Grand Canyon looks its best and they'll give you the same answer: the last hour of the day, when the light goes sideways, the shadows carve the side canyons into relief, and the rock cycles through orange, rose and purple. Most day tours are heading home by then. This one is built backwards from sunset — the day's viewpoints are sequenced so you're standing at the right overlook when the show starts.

You'll still see the full South Rim circuit; you're just seeing its finale the way it deserves to be seen.

What's included

  • Full-day South Rim tour from Sedona, timed for sunset
  • Signature viewpoints plus a chosen sunset overlook
  • Professional guide, park entry handled

Good to know

  • Return to Sedona is after dark — don't book an early dinner that night.
  • Sunset time shifts a lot by season; your confirmed departure time accounts for it.
