Ancient Ruins Jeep Tour

Operated by Pink Jeep Tours · 3 hours · Mild–moderate

About this tour

People lived in these canyons for centuries before anyone called it Sedona. This 3-hour jeep tour heads into the red rock backcountry to the Honanki Heritage Site — one of the largest cliff dwelling sites in the Verde Valley, home to the Sinagua people from about 1150 to 1350 AD — where rooms, pictographs and the shape of a whole community still read clearly in the stone.

It's the thinking traveler's jeep tour: mild-to-moderate roads, a guide strong on archaeology and Native history, and a destination that puts everything else you see in Sedona into a much longer story.

What's included

  • 3-hour guided jeep tour to the Honanki Heritage Site
  • Cliff dwellings and pictographs with expert interpretation
  • Professional local guide

Good to know

  • Ride intensity: mild–moderate — the gentlest route to a major backcountry site.
  • Includes a short walk at the ruins on uneven ground; children as young as 2 can ride (check current listing).
