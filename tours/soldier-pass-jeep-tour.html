Private Soldier Pass Jeep Tour

Private — your group only · Permit-limited trail

About this tour

Soldier Pass is the connoisseur's Sedona jeep trail. The Forest Service strictly limits vehicle permits for it — which keeps it quiet — and the route packs two of Sedona's strangest landmarks into a short, rugged run: the Devil's Kitchen, a massive sinkhole that collapsed in the 1880s and is still growing, and the Seven Sacred Pools, a chain of natural rock basins sacred to the Yavapai-Apache.

Run privately, it's just your group, your guide and a trail most Sedona visitors never get on. Rugged in places, rich in story everywhere.

What's included

  • Private jeep tour of the Soldier Pass Trail — your group only
  • Devil's Kitchen sinkhole and Seven Sacred Pools
  • Professional local guide

Good to know

  • Permit limits make this one of the harder jeep tours to book last-minute — plan ahead.
  • Ride intensity: rugged in sections. Not recommended with back problems or during pregnancy.
