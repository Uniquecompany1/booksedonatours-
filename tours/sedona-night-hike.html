Sedona Night Hike & Stargazing

Evening guided hike + stargazing

About this tour

Most night tours drive you somewhere and sit you down. This one walks you into the red rocks as the light fails — the hour when the stone glows, the desert cools, and the animals come out — then stops in the dark to watch the Milky Way rise over the formations you just hiked through.

It's the night tour for people who'd rather earn the view. Guided the whole way, on established trails, at a pace set for the group.

What's included

  • Guided evening hike on Sedona trails
  • Stargazing stop under Dark Sky skies
  • Local guide

Good to know

  • Real hiking on uneven desert trails — sturdy shoes required, reasonable fitness assumed.
  • Bring a light jacket and water; temperatures drop quickly after sunset.
