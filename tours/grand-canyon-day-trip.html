Grand Canyon Day Trip from Sedona or Flagstaff

9–11 hours · Small group · Free cancellation

About this tour

The workhorse Grand Canyon day trip: a full day from Sedona (or Flagstaff, if that's where you're based) to the South Rim and back, with the drive itself as part of the show — up through Oak Creek Canyon, across the Colorado Plateau, and into the park without touching a parking problem or an entrance line.

At the rim you get the signature viewpoints plus free time in Grand Canyon Village, with a guide narrating the geology and history that turn a view into a story. If you want the classic day trip done properly at a fair price, this is it.

What's included

  • Full-day South Rim tour from Sedona or Flagstaff
  • Multiple viewpoints and Grand Canyon Village time
  • Professional guide and park entry handled
  • Free cancellation

Good to know

  • Day length varies (9–11 hours) with pickup location and season.
  • The rim is ~7,000 feet — bring layers, water and sun protection.
