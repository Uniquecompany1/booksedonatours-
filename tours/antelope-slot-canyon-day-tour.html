Antelope Slot Canyon & Horseshoe Bend Day Tour

11 hours 30 minutes · Small group

About this tour

The small-group version of Sedona's most in-demand day trip. Same two icons — a Navajo-guided walk through Antelope Canyon's sculpted slot walls, and the Horseshoe Bend overlook where the Colorado River bends 270 degrees a thousand feet below — with fewer people in the vehicle and more of your guide's attention to go around.

Departs from Sedona or Flagstaff. The drive north through the Painted Desert is long, which is precisely why the small-group format matters: it's the difference between a bus day and a road trip with a knowledgeable friend.

What's included

  • Round-trip transport from Sedona or Flagstaff
  • Antelope Canyon visit with a Navajo guide
  • Horseshoe Bend overlook
  • Small group size

Good to know

  • About 1.5 miles of walking on uneven ground, with ladders in the canyon. Minimum ages apply.
  • Book early — canyon capacity is controlled and prime dates sell out first.
