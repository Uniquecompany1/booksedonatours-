Red Rock Range Jeep Tour

Operated by Pink Jeep Tours · The gentle one

About this tour

Not every jeep tour has to rattle your fillings. The Red Rock Range is Pink Jeep's mildest ride — two hours on gentle dirt roads through the open range at the mouth of Boynton Canyon, with stops at Taylor Tank and the Bradshaw Overlook and the kind of big-sky views the rugged tours are too busy climbing to enjoy.

It's the right jeep tour for first-timers, nervous riders, photographers, and anyone traveling with grandparents. Book the late departure and the whole western range lights up gold — this is the sunset photographer's pick of the entire jeep catalog.

What's included

  • 2-hour guided jeep tour on mild dirt roads
  • Stops at Taylor Tank and Bradshaw Overlook
  • Professional local guide — geology, plants, wildlife and ranching history

Good to know

  • Ride intensity: mild (2 of 5) — the gentlest jeep tour we sell.
  • Children as young as 2 can ride (check the listing for current age rules).
  • Want more thrill from the same company? Broken Arrow is the famous step up.
