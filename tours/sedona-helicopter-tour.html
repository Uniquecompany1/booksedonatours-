About this tour

Some of Sedona only makes sense from the air. This small-group helicopter flight covers 20+ miles over the landmarks you've been looking up at — Cathedral Rock, Bell Rock, Thunder Mountain, Boynton Canyon and Oak Creek Canyon — and shows you how they all connect. Every seat faces forward, and noise-reducing headsets let you hear the pilot's narration over the rotors.

It holds a perfect 5.0 rating, and reviewers keep saying the same thing: the front seats are worth it, and it's the splurge they'd do again.
