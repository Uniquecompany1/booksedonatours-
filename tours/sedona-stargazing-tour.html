Sedona Stargazing Tour

★★★★☆ 4.3 from 994 reviews · Operated by Sedona Stargazing Tours

About this tour

Sedona earned its International Dark Sky Community designation by keeping its lights low — and the reward is a night sky most people have never actually seen. This tour takes you to a dedicated observing site outside town, where guides set up telescopes and walk you through the constellations, planets and deep-sky objects overhead.

At about 75 minutes, it's an easy add to any Sedona evening: dinner in town, then the universe. Nearly a thousand guests have rated it, and the guides' knowledge is the consistent theme in the reviews.

What's included

  • Guided stargazing with telescopes at a dark observing site
  • Expert astronomy guides
  • Free cancellation

Good to know

  • Darkest skies fall around the new moon — if your dates are flexible, aim for it.
  • Book your first clear night, not your last, so weather leaves you a backup.
  • Desert nights get cold even in summer — bring a jacket.
