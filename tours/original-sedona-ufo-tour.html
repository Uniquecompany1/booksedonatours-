The Original Sedona UFO & Stargazing Night Tour

1 hour 45 minutes · Free cancellation

About this tour

Sedona's UFO scene is real culture here — decades of reported sightings over the red rocks, and a small industry of night-sky guides who know every satellite schedule and flight path so they can show you the things that don't match either. This is the original tour that started it: part stargazing, part storytelling, part genuine sky-watching with guides who take the debunking as seriously as the mystery.

Believer, skeptic or just curious — it's one of the most entertaining evenings in Sedona, under some of the darkest skies in America.

What's included

  • 1 hour 45 minutes of guided night-sky and UFO watching
  • Guides who identify satellites, planes and planets — and flag what they can't identify
  • Free cancellation

Good to know

  • Bring a jacket — desert nights cool fast, even in summer.
  • Prefer pure astronomy? The Sedona Stargazing Tour is telescope-focused.
