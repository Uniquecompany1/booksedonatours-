Private Medicine Wheel & Vortex Journey

About this tour

Four private hours built around one of the oldest tools in North American spiritual practice: the medicine wheel, a framework for looking at your life in its four directions and seasons. A practitioner-guide leads the ceremony and the journey through Sedona's vortex sites at a pace that leaves room for the work to actually land.

Because it's private, it shapes itself around what you bring to it — grief, transition, gratitude, questions. People book this at turning points, and guides who do this work are chosen for exactly that.

What's included

  • Private 4-hour journey — your group only
  • Medicine wheel ceremony with a practitioner-guide
  • Visits to Sedona vortex sites

Good to know
