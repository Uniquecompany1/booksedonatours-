Broken Arrow / Scenic Rim Combo Tour

Operated by Pink Jeep Tours · 3 hours · The greatest-hits tour

About this tour

The two most popular Pink Jeep routes, back to back in one 3-hour tour: the Broken Arrow rock-crawl — Chicken Point, Submarine Rock, the Road of No Return — followed by the Scenic Rim climb to the 2,000-foot overlooks above the valley.

If you have one jeep tour in you and want the full range of what Sedona off-roading is — the technical slickrock and the huge views — this is the efficient answer. It's also the best value per experience in the Pink Jeep catalog.

What's included

  • 3-hour combined tour: Broken Arrow Trail + Scenic Rim
  • Stops at Chicken Point, Submarine Rock and the rim overlooks
  • Professional local guide

Good to know

  • Ride intensity: bumpy to rugged on the Broken Arrow section. Not recommended with back or neck problems, or during pregnancy.
  • Books out earlier than either single tour — reserve ahead in spring and fall.
