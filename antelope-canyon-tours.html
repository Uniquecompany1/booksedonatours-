Antelope Canyon & Horseshoe Bend

Two of the most photographed places on Earth, in one day from Sedona.

Antelope Canyon sits on Navajo land near Page, Arizona, and can only be visited with a Navajo guide — which is exactly why a day trip from Sedona makes sense: the driving, the permits and the timing are handled, and Horseshoe Bend is ten minutes from the canyon. Know before you book

  • It's a long day: roughly 10–11.5 hours door to door. The payoff is worth it, but don't schedule a dinner reservation for 6pm.
  • Walking is required: about 1.5 miles over uneven surfaces, with ladders inside the canyon. Not suitable for infants; minimum ages apply per tour.
  • Upper vs. Lower: Lower Antelope is narrower, more adventurous and usually less crowded; Upper is wider, flatter and famous for midday light beams. Weather can swap sections — operators advise at booking.
  • Book early: canyon entry is capacity-controlled by the Navajo Nation and prime dates go first.
